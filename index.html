About our internal tool: shorts-pipeline

shorts-pipeline is a private production tool that runs on the operator's own computer. It is not a public app and is not offered to anyone else.

  • It is used only for the operator's own two YouTube channels listed above.
  • It drafts a script, produces the video, and runs automatic quality checks.
  • Every video is reviewed and approved by the operator before it is uploaded. Nothing is published without that approval.
  • About two videos per day in total, at most one per channel per day.
  • Every upload is marked as altered or synthetic (AI-generated) content, as YouTube requires.
  • It reads basic analytics for the operator's own channels only, to review how videos perform.

How the tool uses Google data is described in our Privacy Policy.
